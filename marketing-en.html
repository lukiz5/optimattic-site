The core

99 purchases according to Meta 22 confirmed by payment

Ads Manager shows a purchase count. Your payment system shows deposits. Almost nobody puts those two numbers side by side, because they sit in different tools and are counted differently. That is why I start with measurement, not with campaigns.

Which one are you

I do not promise that Meta will start showing exactly what you have in the bank. That is not achievable, and anyone who promises it either does not understand how attribution works or is counting on you not checking. I promise something else: you will see both numbers separately and you will know where the gap comes from.

A

Subscription, membership, repeat purchase

Your problem does not end at acquisition. A customer pays once, and then either comes back every month or quietly drops out - most often because a payment failed, not because they decided to leave.

  • cost per acquisition counted from the first real payment
  • how much revenue drops out on failed payments and how much of it comes back
  • which campaigns bring customers who stay

One thing said plainly: your payment provider has its own retry logic and will recover part of it on its own. I account for what gets recovered above that baseline, and I show both numbers.

This is me
B

Creators selling their own product

Sales are coming in, but mostly from people who already know you. Organic reach does the work, so do comments and direct messages, and nobody can say how much of it is the ads and how much is your audience.

  • revenue split between organic reach and ads, in numbers
  • reaching beyond your own followers without breaking what already works
  • a full list of material to record each month

One honest point: if your sales come mainly from organic reach, ads will not double them. They will add growth on top of what would have come anyway, and they will show you where you are losing money. I measure that added growth and report it separately. Anyone promising more is guessing.
