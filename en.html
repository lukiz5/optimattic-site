Questions before we talk

Where do we start?

With a 15-minute call about one process your team handles manually every day. If AI makes sense there, I suggest an audit of that process. If it doesn't, I say so right away.

How long does implementation take?

The first working system usually takes a few weeks. We start with one process and add more when the first produces a measurable result.

Do we need to change our tools?

Usually not. I connect to what you already use: email, CRM, your online store and spreadsheets.

What if AI doesn't make sense for us?

I will tell you on our first call. An implementation without a measurable result is not worthwhile for either side.
